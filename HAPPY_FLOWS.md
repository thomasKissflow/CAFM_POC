# Demo flows

Five flows. Each takes 2–5 minutes. Switch person with the avatar menu, top right.

**Before you start:** open the app, check the header says **Live · Kissflow**. The demo building is Qamar Residences. There are 5 open jobs.

---

## 1. The resident call (the headline)

**Be:** Layla Al Suwaidi (Resident) · **Go to:** Talk

1. Press **Start talking** and allow the microphone.
2. Say: *"My AC in the living room is blowing warm air since last night."*
3. It asks one thing at a time. Answer normally. Interrupt it mid-sentence — it stops and listens.
4. Say yes when it reads the request back.

**Point at:** the job card filling in as she speaks; the priority jumping to P2 for summer; **DLP: contractor liable**; the reference number it reads out, which the system generated, not the AI.

*Switch to Admin → AI conversations to show the transcript and the summary written by itself.*

---

## 2. Liability decided the moment a request arrives

**Be:** Layla (Resident) · **Go to:** New request

1. Pick **AC not cooling**, write a line, send.
2. Open **Work orders** as Sarah Whitfield (FM manager).

**Point at:** the new job at the top, already carrying priority, liability and a running clock — nobody typed those. Open it: the liability banner says who pays and how many days are left in the defects period.

---

## 3. A job through the technician's phone

**Be:** Rashid Qureshi (Subcontractor supervisor) · **Go to:** Work orders

1. Open **AC dripping water** (Apt 1305). It's waiting for the subcontractor. Press **Accept**.
2. Switch to **Joel Santos (Technician)**. The job is now in Today's jobs.
3. Open it: **On site** → tick the checklist → add a photo → pick the fault cause → **Complete job**.
4. Switch to **Arjun Menon (Helpdesk)** → open the job → **Verify & close**.

**Point at:** the phone screen is the same app; every step is timestamped in the activity log; the fault cause decides who pays.

---

## 4. The money: charging it back to the subcontractor

**Be:** Khalid Al Hammadi (DLP manager) · **Go to:** DLP & recovery

**Point at:** recovered, pending and disputed money; the split per subcontractor; the defects-period clock for the building.

Click the work order on any back-charge row: the photos, the fault cause and the timestamped log are all on the job, so the charge can be defended rather than argued. As DLP manager you can press **Mark recovered** on a row and watch the ledger move.

*If you closed a job in flow 3, its back-charge is at the top of the list.*

---

## 5. The 60-second executive tour

**Be:** Hamdan Al Falasi (Executive) · **Go to:** Command centre

**Point at:** money recovered year to date; jobs closest to breaching their SLA; the batch-defect alert (the same actuator failing across one batch of AC units, all by one subcontractor); then **Client report** — the monthly report writes itself from the same records.

---

## 6. Make it their building (30 seconds, in front of them)

**Be:** Nadia Farouk (Admin) · **Go to:** Branding, then Properties

1. **Branding** — type the client's name, drop in their logo, pick their brand colour. Every screen changes as you type; press Save and it is there for good.
2. **Properties** — add one of their towers: name it, say when it was handed over and for how long the contractor carries the defects. Set the floors and how many apartments each has, tick "fit every apartment with a fan coil", pick the plant it has.
3. **Add property.** A few seconds later it is in Kissflow.
4. Open **Portfolio** — their tower is on the register, with its liability clock already counting down. Raise a request against it and it comes back "contractor liable".

*Point to make:* nothing here was prepared for them this morning. The estate is theirs to type in, and the liability maths follows from the handover date.
